# The 2.709-day eclipse near TIC 349414709

**The eclipse signal was published in 2024 under TIC 349414705.** IJspeert et al. give a TESS year-3 QLP period of 2.7084875683 d and a reference eclipse at BTJD 2232.0149571. The present year-3 light curve places an eclipse at BTJD 2232.0130841, 2.70 minutes earlier. The 2.7091725 d recurrence measured over a longer baseline differs by 59.18 seconds per cycle. The same catalogue row gives 5.4170243594 d for year 1, consistent with the double-period alias. Source attribution within this crowded aperture is a distinct scientific question. [IJspeert et al.
